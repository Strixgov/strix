How it works · Act 1 of 2

A clinician submits a patient note.
Strix governs the action before the model sees it.

1

Intake

Dr. Rivera submits to the care-portal. Raw note contains patient name, DOB, MRN, clinical observations.

2

Classify

The kernel identifies PHI fields and applies HIPAA Safe Harbor redaction (§164.514(b)(2)): patient name redacted, DOB bucketed to a 10-year age range, MRN tokenized.

3

Evaluate + Sign

Policy evaluates the redacted action. If allowed, Strix Ed25519-signs the SE v1 canonical payload. The receipt is final before the model ever sees the redacted payload.

4

Verify

Any party — regulator, BAA partner, internal auditor — can cryptographically verify the receipt using npx @strixgov/verifier. No Strix infrastructure in the trust path.

See the full clinical workflow run locally on your machine: npx @strixgov/healthcare-demo

Regulatory mapping

Three frameworks. One receipt. Every flag derived, never asserted.

Strix doesn't let the producer claim compliance. The signing host can't write article28_audit_ready: true into a record and have the verifier honor it. Every flag below is derived by the verifier from independently-checkable cryptographic evidence — that asymmetry is the load-bearing trust property.

Framework Article / § Derived flag Verification primitives required
EU AI Act Article 12 article12_traceable trace fields present + hash valid
Article 12 article12_tamper_resistant hash + chain + signature all valid
Article 14 article14_oversight_supported trace fields present
Article 28 article28_audit_ready all of the above
HIPAA Technical Safeguards · 45 CFR §164.312 §164.312(b) hipaa_audit_log_present signature present + trace fields present
§164.312(c)(1) hipaa_integrity_assured signature valid (authenticates canonical bytes)
§164.312(d) hipaa_access_authenticated actor identified + signature valid

Strix asserts only the three HIPAA Technical Safeguards that derive from cryptographic evidence. Administrative safeguards (§164.308) and Physical safeguards (§164.310) are covered-entity responsibilities outside the per-record evidence surface. Workforce training and workstation security require organizational programs, not cryptographic primitives.

Where this lives in your stack

Three healthcare AI patterns Strix governs today

Revenue Cycle Management

AI agents handling eligibility checks, prior-auth, denial management, appeals. Every payer-facing action signs a receipt with the actor's role, the patient's de-identified eligibility, and the policy decision. Audit-ready before the auditor asks.

capability: rcm.eligibility.check

Medicaid Enrollment Platforms

Auto-fill, eligibility determination, application submission to state systems. Strix signs each state-facing call with the applicant's de-identified profile and the verified third-party data sources used. Improper-payment audits become a verifier query.

capability: medicaid.application.submit

Clinical Decision Support

AI suggestions for diagnosis, medication, treatment paths. The agent's recommendation is signed alongside the de-identified case context and the model version that produced it. Reproducibility, transparency, and Article 14 oversight in one cryptographic receipt.

capability: clinical.decision.suggest
